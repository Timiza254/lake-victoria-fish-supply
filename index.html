About Us

Who we are

Lake Victoria Fish Supply connects fish traders, hotels, restaurants, supermarkets, institutions and wholesalers directly with fishermen from Port Victoria on Lake Victoria. We built this business to close the gap between the lake and buyers across Kenya who need fish they can actually trust.

Our mission is simple: make it easy for any business in Kenya to receive genuinely fresh fish, no matter how far they are from the lake.

✓

Why choose us: we inspect and grade every order before it leaves Port Victoria.

✓

Freshness commitment: fish is packed in ice within hours of landing, not days.

✓

Quality commitment: we work only with fishermen we know and trust.
