About Us

From the fishermen of Port Victoria, straight to your door

We connect fish traders, hotels, restaurants, supermarkets and processors directly with fishermen from Port Victoria on Lake Victoria. Every order is inspected for quality, packed with ice to lock in freshness, and dispatched safely to its destination through trusted passenger transport companies operating across Kenya.

No middlemen markups, no guesswork on freshness — just a straight line from the lake to your business.
