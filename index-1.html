How We Get Your Fish to You

1

Source

We source fresh fish directly from fishermen around Lake Victoria.

2

Fish is sorted according to type, size and customer's requirements.

3

Orders are packed properly with ice to help maintain freshness during transportation.

4

Dispatch

The parcel is handed over to the customer's preferred transport company.

5

Receive

The customer receives the fish at the agreed destination and transport stage.
